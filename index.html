Problem & flow

Why we’re building BillMyFlat & how it will fit your cycle

Our goal is to replace scattered Excel sheets, WhatsApp threads and manual follow-ups with one predictable, transparent system.

Current pain

Manual, repetitive and opaque

Most committees juggle Excel files, ad-hoc formulas and WhatsApp messages. Residents see different versions of numbers, and disputes start around “who paid what & when”.

Our approach

One source of truth

BillMyFlat will keep billing logic, invoices and payment tracking in one place. Everyone — treasurer, secretary and residents — sees the same consistent data.

Planned monthly flow

Set up → generate → track

Configure your society once, generate bills for all flats in a click, then track payments and send reminders without copy-paste work every month.

Designed around transparency & fewer disputes

Every rupee should be traceable. Every resident should see the same numbers as the committee.

  • Consistent, system-generated invoices instead of one-off Excel exports.
  • Clear mapping of each payment to a flat, invoice and month.
  • Exportable, audit-ready reports for AGMs and society elections.
  • Built to reduce “who paid what” arguments before they even start.
